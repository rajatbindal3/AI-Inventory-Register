# AI-Inventory-Register
The AI Inventory Register is a structured document that records every AI system operating within an organization. Think of it as a master census or a "source of truth" for all AI assets. It i the starting point of the AI Governance.
These templates demonstrate my ability to operationalize EU AI Act compliance requirements.

## Templates Included

| Template | Purpose | EU AI Act Reference |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **AI Inventory Register** | Track and classify AI systems by risk tier | Annex III (High-Risk Classification) |
